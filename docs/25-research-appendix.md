## 2. Patterns by category

### 2.1 Digital yearbook products

**The Yearbook** — https://theyearbookapp.com/students ✅
The most relevant real reference: a yearbook app whose student roster screen is built like a book.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display serif, italics used) + Karla (grotesque for UI/body).
- **Structure:** fixed left sidebar; nav grouped under **semantic section labels** — "Remember
  everyone", "Our year", "People", "School life", "Collect & organize".
- **Hierarchy:** breadcrumb → muted eyebrow ("People · Your student roster") → serif H1
  ("Students") → muted sub-line ("Every name belongs. Every story starts with someone.").
- **What works:** reads as a printed artefact's table of contents; the serif/sans pairing carries
  "editorial" without foil-and-navy kitsch; muted micro-type signals seriousness.
- **What feels dated:** a risk of cold CMS feel — the whole thing leans on Cormorant Garamond doing
  the emotional work.

**Jostens Yearbook+** — https://www.jostens.com/yearbooks/advisers-and-staff/technology-and-experience/yearbook-plus ◐
"…embedding each student's portrait image with a unique digital identity… generated by tagging the
pixels of the portrait, allowing the image to function like a QR code," explicitly "does not use
facial recognition."
**Convention:** the physical portrait is the identity anchor; digital is an *overlay* on the printed
page, never a replacement.

**Picaboo Creator Studio** — https://www.picabooyearbooks.com/yearbook-software ◐
Browser-based yearbook design software with professional templates, team management, photo tools.
**Convention:** page templates + palette/asset kits. Schools buy *finished books*, not apps.

**Classmates yearbook collection** — https://www.classmates.com/yearbooks/ ✅
- **Type:** Albert Sans alone — deliberately neutral.
- **Copy:** "Classmates.com has over 470,000 high school yearbooks online. Search our yearbook
  collection for free or purchase a high-quality yearbook reprint."
- **What works:** browse-first, no-login front door; nostalgia monetised through the *reprint*.
- **What feels cheap:** generic directory aesthetic; zero ceremony around the object.

### 2.2 School / class / alumni apps

**Classmates** — https://explore.classmates.com/ ✅/◐
"largest online collections of digitized high school yearbooks… search for your school and
graduation year to browse yearbook pages, see class photos, revisit memories… and even order
physical reprints."
**Pattern:** *search by school + graduation year is the entry ritual*; the yearbook is the keepsake;
the profile is the social object.

**MyAlumni.network (Vaave)** — https://www.myalumni.network/ ◐
Stated loop: search alumni → view profiles → start conversations → connect. That is the entire
feature set. **A class directory is conventionally a search-and-profile product, not a keepsake
product.**

**Convention:** alumni apps are B2B directory tooling with faceless list UI. Nobody has made this
beautiful — which is why a browsable, paged yearbook would be genuinely differentiated.

### 2.3 Digital scrapbook / keepsake

**Dear Scrapbook** — https://www.dearscrapbook.com/ ✅
- **Type:** Poppins (400–800) for UI + **Caveat** for handwriting + Archivo Black for impact.
- **Colour:** primary `#d6295c` (saturated rose/magenta), text `#23222a`, white ground, plus soft
  palette tints used as page/card backgrounds.
- **Shape language:** polaroid frames, washi-tape strips at frame corners, and **cards deliberately
  misaligned** — `transform:translateY(-14px) scale(1.03) rotate(-7deg)`, `rotate(1.75deg)`.
- **Content model:** 20 themes, 50 handwriting fonts, tape/pins/staples/seals/glue dots ("make
  photos look properly stuck on"), plus a global **"Open Theme to restyle every page at once"**.
- **Signature moment:** the *theme switch*.
- **What works:** the tape/pin/glue vocabulary does physicality cheaply and convincingly; the
  deliberate rotation reads as handmade rather than as a bug.
- **What feels cheap:** saturated pink + Archivo Black + glitter-adjacent stickers is the 2019
  Etsy/Pinterest scrapbook look; high decoration with no typographic hierarchy.

**CraftGossip — "Digital Scrapbooking Is Back: 12 Apps…" (2026-08-12)** ✅
https://scrapbooking.craftgossip.com/digital-scrapbooking-is-back-12-apps-for-creating-scrapbook-pages-on-your-ipad/
- Trend set: "digital film strips, torn-paper edges, handwritten notes, scanned ticket stubs,
  postage stamps, doodles, vintage textures and photographs arranged to look slightly imperfect."
- **"The secret is not making everything too perfect."** "Allow photographs to overlap slightly.
  Turn one image a few degrees instead of lining up every edge. Layer torn-paper textures behind
  your photos and add tape, stitching, ink marks or handwritten journaling."
- Material rule: scan real craft supplies — "a scrap of your grandmother's fabric, a child's
  drawing, an old envelope, pressed flowers, handwritten recipes or a faded ticket… always more
  interesting than a perfectly matched set of generic digital embellishments."
- On Goodnotes: "Your handwriting remains part of the finished page, which gives the layout the
  warmth that highly polished temp[lates]" lack.
- **Project Life App** — "pocket-style layouts… clean, structured grids" (the *ordered* extreme).
  **Nooka** — freeform "personal art journals rather than rigid photo grids." **STCKRZ** — "bright,
  nostalgic and intentionally a little overdecorated—in the best possible way."

### 2.4 Handwriting / doodle / shared annotation

**You Doodle** (Digital Ruby) — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/you-doodle-draw-on-photos/id517871755 ◐
Draw on photos, add text, share. Play listing mentions "Doodling on a friend."
**Pattern:** doodle = playful tool, not keepsake.

**Collaborative whiteboards** ◐
Excalidraw = "minimalistic hand-drawn diagrams"; Miro = all-purpose brainstorming; FigJam = design
integration; tldraw = open-source; whiteboard.fi = education.
**Pattern:** hand-drawn rendering lowers the stakes of a *working* tool; it does not signal
preciousness. Excalidraw's charm comes from rough.js imperfection and near-zero chrome.

**Digital guestbooks** — https://weddingsguestbook.com/ ◐
"The digital wedding guest book and photo booth in one iPad app. Guests leave handwritten notes,
photos, and voice messages — no wifi needed — and you get a designed PDF keepsake. One-time price."
Guestlense / Guestpix / Fotify are QR-based, "no app downloads required."
**Pattern:** the host owns the artefact; guests contribute in 20 seconds; the output is a designed
document.

**Honest gap:** no consumer social app where many people draw on one shared object and it persists
as a precious keepsake. Scribbl's ritual sits between the guestbook and Excalidraw.

## 3. The yearbook page tradition (concrete)

**Touchwall, "Yearbook Page Layouts: Template Guide for Editors"** ✅
https://touchwall.us/blog/yearbook-page-layouts-template-guide-editors-designing-every-section/
**Jostens School Annual, "10) Creating Page Layouts"** ✅
https://support.schoolannual.com/hc/en-us/articles/115011280867-10-Creating-Page-Layouts

- **Format:** 8.5×11 portrait, read as **spreads**. "Design and evaluate eve[n]…"; the design
  unit is the two-page spread.
- **Margins (verbatim):** outside/fore-edge 0.375"–0.5"; inside/gutter 0.5"–0.75" ("Account for
  binding loss"); top/head 0.375"–0.5"; bottom/foot 0.5"–0.625" ("Folio line lives here").
- **Grid:** **six-column grid** by default — "columns can combine into two-column photo blocks,
  three-column text runs, or six-column full-bleed images — while maintaining consistency across
  section types." Plus a **baseline grid** matched to body leading.
- **Gutter width carries density:** 0.125" for dense portrait sections; 0.25"–0.375" for feature
  spreads where white space is intentional.
- **Reading path:** the eye "enters at the top-left of a spread, moves right, and needs a clear
  path through the content." Balance is **optical**, not mathematical: "a heavy photo on the left
  needs a strong graphic element on the right."
- **Section template taxonomy (Jostens, verbatim):** candid, club and organization, sports,
  portrait, advertising, recognition. Touchwall adds opening spread, portrait/class pages, senior
  feature spreads, academics/clubs, athletics, faculty & staff, closing spread & index.
- **"Theme Carrier Pages — Making Every Spread Feel Like The Same Book."** A repeated themed device
  (a rule, a colour, a motif, a running head) recurs at every section boundary. **This is the
  single most transferable idea for a screen-based yearbook.**
- **Hierarchy is spatial, not typographic** — TreeRing, https://www.treering.com/post/yearbook-design-hierarchy
  (title verified; body did not render).

## 4. Recurring design lessons

1. **A repeated "theme carrier" device beats one-off decoration.** Touchwall.
2. **Design spreads, not pages.** Touchwall.
3. **Density is a section-level decision.** Touchwall.
4. **Hierarchy = arrangement in order of importance; the reader must know where to begin and where
   to exit.** TreeRing.
5. **Imperfection is the warmth signal.** CraftGossip.
6. **Real material > generic embellishment.** CraftGossip.
7. **Handwriting as the warmth carrier.** CraftGossip on Goodnotes.
8. **Physical attachment vocabulary is cheap and convincing:** tape, pins, staples, glue dots,
   seals, stitching. Dear Scrapbook.
9. **Global theme control = the signature moment.** Dear Scrapbook; Jostens design libraries
   (https://www.jostens.com/yearbooks/advisers-and-staff/yearbook-inspiration/yearbook-design-libraries).
10. **Serial display + neutral grotesque is the proven "editorial but warm" pairing.** The Yearbook.
11. **Low-friction contribution beats an app download.** weddingsguestbook.com; Guestlense.
12. **Digital must be an overlay on the physical, not a replacement.** Jostens Yearbook+.
13. **Nostalgia is monetised through the physical reprint, not the app.** Classmates.

## 5. 2024–26 trend notes

- **"The Return of Analog" (trend #1 of 2025).** Shortcut's UX/UI 2025 writeup opens with a
  "rebuttonization renaissance" — physical dials, knobs and buttons returning after "touchscreen
  mania"; Hipstamatic's virtual knobs, Fujifilm X-Pro3's dials, Apple re-embracing physical
  controls. https://www.shortcut.io/news-events/the-ux-ui-trends-defining-2025-nostalgic-chaotic-and-personal ✅
- **Neomorphism 2.0** — Monzo/Lunar/Revolut and Microsoft illustration as carriers. Same ✅
- **Warm neutrals + organic shapes + bento containers**, verbatim: "With warm neutrals and organic
  shapes, these designs whisper, 'Relax—you've had enough chaos in your inbox today'… rounded
  elements, fluid animations and containers (Bento and others)… encouraging users to linger without
  overwhelming them." Same ✅ — *the closest published endorsement of the warm instinct, but framed
  as rounded/soft, not sketchy/paper.*
- **"Textural & Analog Revival: Handmade Design Trends for 2025"**, Dirk Petzold, We And The Color,
  2025-08-09 — "2025's top design trend… handmade aesthetics are bringing a human touch to digital
  media." https://weandthecolor.com/textural-analog-revival-why-the-future-of-digital-media-is-handmade/205096
  (title + metadata only)
- **Scrapbook nostalgia as a product category** — CraftGossip, Aug 2026: photo booths,
  disposable-camera effects, film strips, postage stamps, handwritten diary pages are all "back." ✅

**Practical read:** the trend is **textural and material**, not **sketchy and cartoonish**. The
differentiating detail in 2026 is *rotation, overlap, tape, grain, real handwriting* — not doodled
borders and hand-drawn icons on every control.

## 6. What did NOT work

1. **2020-era neomorphism** (soft plastic) — now "Neomorphism 2.0"; the original wave is dated.
   Shortcut.
2. **Perfectly matched sets of generic digital embellishment** — produce pages that feel
   computer-generated. CraftGossip.
3. **Over-decoration** — STCKRZ is the explicit label: "intentionally a little overdecorated—in the
   best possible way," workable only because its restraint elsewhere (grid, type) is firm.
4. **High-saturation + heavy-display-type scrapbook** (Dear Scrapbook's `#d6295c` + Archivo Black) —
   the cheap reading of "handmade"; dates the product to a Pinterest-era aesthetic. ○ inference
   from the page's own palette and sticker volume, not a quoted critique.
5. **A directory-as-yearbook** — the alumni-app norm (search, filter, profile card, message button)
   is what a class roster feels like when it isn't designed as an object. This is what `/explore`
   risks feeling like.
6. **Digitising the print book's typography literally** — navy/gold foil is a print-cover
   convention; at screen scale it reads as costume. The print tradition's transferable parts are
   grid, folio, theme-carrier and section template — not metallic gradients.
7. **"Cream paper + hand-drawn sketch" as a full-surface treatment** — signals "handmade" without
   saying anything specific. ○ inference, flagged.
