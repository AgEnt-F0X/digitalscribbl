Design the profile page of the Scribbl app - the page where a student opens a classmate's signed T-shirt. This is a redesign of an existing page. Mobile-first, 390px wide, scrollable. It uses the new design system, so the chrome is quiet and typographic and the T-shirt is the only lit, elevated, saturated object on the screen.

TOP BAR, sticky, 64px tall:
- Left: the wordmark Scribbl in the display serif, 22px
- Right: a notification bell as a thin-line icon (not an emoji, no bell glyph), then a 32px circular avatar
- A 1px hairline rule underneath. No Explore link on mobile.

HERO, centred, generous space:
- The classmate's avatar wearing their white T-shirt, large, on a flat paper ground with ONE soft shadow under it. This is the only elevated object on the page.
- Below the shirt, the name AARAV SHARMA in the display serif, 28px, centred
- Below the name, one line of small tracked uppercase monospace, muted: B.TECH COMPUTER SCIENCE & ENGINEERING / CLASS OF 2026
- Below that, one small tracked uppercase monospace line, muted: SCRIBBLED BY 17 FRIENDS
- Below that, a single primary button, full width, 44px tall, near-black ground: Sign this shirt
- NO sticker notes floating around the avatar, NO drifting doodles, NO whiteboard, NO illustrated campus background, NO halo, NO doodle or emoji anywhere on this page.

THE SHIRT SECTION, a full-width section below the hero, separated by a 1px hairline rule, with 32px of space above the heading:
- A section heading in small tracked uppercase monospace: THE SHIRT
- On the right of the heading, two small text tabs side by side, tracked uppercase monospace: SHIRT 1 and SHIRT 2, with the active one underlined in near-black
- Below the heading, a single row of two small tracked uppercase monospace tabs: FRONT and BACK, the active one underlined
- Below the tabs, the T-shirt itself, large, centred, on a white card with a 1px border and square corners, covered in visible marker marks in the marker colours - a blue handwritten scrawl, a red heart, a green doodle, an orange star. The marks are drawn ON the white fabric, inside the shirt outline, never on the card.
- Under the card, a row of three small tracked uppercase monospace facts separated by space: 24 MARKS / 17 FRIENDS / SHIRT 1 OF 2

THE SHIRT MAP, a section below with a 1px hairline rule above it:
- A section heading in small tracked uppercase monospace: HOW MUCH OF THE SHIRT IS SIGNED
- Two thin progress rows, each a label and a 4px tall bar. Label in body sans 13px on the left, percentage in small tracked uppercase monospace on the right. FRONT - 62%. BACK - 28%. The filled part of each bar is near-black, the rest a light neutral. No rounded pill ends, no gradients.

THE YEARBOOK CARD, a section below with a 1px hairline rule above it:
- A section heading in small tracked uppercase monospace: IN THE YEARBOOK
- A white card with a 1px border and square corners containing: the name AARAV SHARMA in the body sans, 15px, semibold; beneath it one line in small tracked uppercase monospace, muted: B.TECH CSE / CLASS OF 2026; beneath that one line of body sans 14px: Will be printed in the Class of 2026 volume in December. And a small flat text link at the bottom right of the card: SEE A PREVIEW

RECENT ACTIVITY, a section below with a 1px hairline rule above it:
- A section heading in small tracked uppercase monospace: RECENT ACTIVITY
- Four rows, each a single line: on the left a 36px square avatar thumbnail, then the name in body sans 14px semibold, then the event in body sans 14px muted - MEGHNA NAIR signed your shirt, ARJUN MENON signed your shirt, MAYANK IYER signed your shirt, ROHAN BHATT signed your shirt. A hairline rule under each row.

FOOTER, after a 48px gap:
- One line of small tracked uppercase monospace, centred, muted: REPORT AN ISSUE
- One line of small tracked uppercase monospace, centred, muted: SCRIBBL / CLASS OF 2026

STRICT CONSTRAINTS - follow the design system exactly:

- Use ONLY the fonts, colors, spacing, radius, shadow and component styles defined in the design system. Do not introduce any fonts, colors, or visual styles not in the design system.
- The page ground is the light paper tone from the design system. Cards are white with a 1px border and square corners. There are three shadows in the system and only ONE is used on this page: the soft lift under the hero T-shirt.
- The marker colours appear on the fabric only. They are never used for a button, a badge, a link or an accent.
- Gold appears nowhere on this page.
- Section separation is by a 1px hairline rule and 32px of space, never by a divider ornament, a hand-drawn asterisk, a row of dots or a centred ornament.
- All section headings are small tracked uppercase monospace. There is exactly ONE display serif element on the page: the name AARAV SHARMA.
- No emoji, no doodles, no sticker notes, no tape, no washi, no paper texture, no illustration, no gradients, no rounded blob shapes, no hand-drawn borders, no sketch radii, no pill buttons.
- Spacing uses only 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96. Radius is 0 or 2. Type roles are only the ones in the design system.
