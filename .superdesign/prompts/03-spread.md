Design ONE interior spread of the Scribbl Class of 2026 yearbook. This is the signature page of the whole product: an autograph page where every portrait cell is a classmate's signed T-shirt instead of a photograph. Desktop, 1280px wide, two facing pages of a bound book, cream paper stock.

LAYOUT:

Two facing pages side by side with a vertical gutter hairline between them.

RUNNING HEAD, above both pages: a single row with 'CSE - CLASS OF 2026' in small tracked uppercase monospace on the far left, a section marker '§ 02' in small tracked uppercase monospace in gold on the far right, and a 1px hairline rule beneath the row spanning the full width.

LEFT PAGE - THE FEATURE:
- A large square-ish plate with a 1px border and a slightly deeper cream mat, containing ONE white T-shirt illustration seen from the front, filling most of the plate. The shirt carries three or four visible signed marks in marker colours - a short handwritten scrawl, a small doodle, a heart outline, a sticker shape. The marks are the point: they must be clearly visible against the white fabric.
- Beneath the plate, a caption row separated by a 1px rule: on the left 'AARAV SHARMA' in the body sans, semibold, 14px; on the right 'SHIRT 1 OF 2 · 24 MARKS · 17 FRIENDS' in small tracked uppercase monospace, muted.
- Below the caption, one short line of body text, max 30 words, in the body sans: 'Signed by everyone who still owed him a message. He signed theirs back in blue.'

RIGHT PAGE - THE PORTRAIT GRID:
- A 2-column by 3-row grid of six cells with a consistent 24px gutter. Every cell is identical: a 3:4 portrait plate with a 1px border, containing a small white T-shirt illustration with two or three marker marks on it, each shirt slightly different from its neighbours. No photographs, no faces, no avatars, no icons.
- Under each cell, the classmate's name in small tracked uppercase monospace, and under that a 12px body-sans programme line.
- Use these six, in this order: AARAV SHARMA / B.Tech CSE; MEGHNA NAIR / B.Tech CSE; ARJUN MENON / B.Tech CSE; MAYANK IYER / B.Tech CSE; DIYA PRASAD / B.Tech CSE; ROHAN BHATT / B.Tech CSE.
- All six cells are exactly the same size. This is a directory, not a scrapbook. Do not vary cell size, do not feature one cell larger.

PAGE FOLIOS: a small tracked uppercase monospace page number in the outer top corner of each page - one at the far left of the left page, one at the far right of the right page.

STRICT CONSTRAINTS - follow the design system exactly:

- Use ONLY the fonts, colors, spacing, radius, shadow and component styles defined in the design system. Do not introduce any fonts, colors, or visual styles not in the design system.
- The pages are cream paper stock. The text is near-black ink. Gold appears on exactly ONE thing on this spread: the § 02 section marker. Nothing else is gold.
- The marker colours on the shirts are the only saturated colour in the spread. They are content, never UI accents.
- Hairlines and one flat 1px border per plate. No drop shadows, no gradients, no rounded corners on plates, no stickers floating outside the grid, no tape, no washi, no paper texture, no hand-drawn outlines, no doodles in the margins, no emoji.
- The shirts are drawn as clean flat vector shapes in the design system ink colour on a white ground. They are not photographs and not illustrations of people.
- Type roles only: body sans for the two body lines and the programme lines, small tracked uppercase monospace for the name lines, the running head, the caption right-hand text and the folios. No display serif on this spread.
- Spacing uses only 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96. Radius: plates and cells are square, 0 radius.
